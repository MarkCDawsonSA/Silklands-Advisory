Experience on every side of the table

I've negotiated the deals, put capital behind them and been the executive accountable for delivering them, working alongside strong management teams and inspirational, driven founders. I've held more than 150 directorships, from group main boards to development and finance SPVs, including joint venture and investment entities, so I understand boards from both the executive and the oversight side.

A decade on the main board of Vita Group

As Executive Director and Chief Legal Officer I helped build a platform of 29 student buildings (11,000+ beds) in 17 cities across the UK and Spain, plus c.3,500 co-living and BTR units. Assets were sold to DWS, AXA, Mapletree, Savills IM and Morgan Stanley, and I set up and ran joint ventures with Bruntwood, Greater Manchester Pension Fund and Metrovacesa.

Group CEO of a PE-backed legal services group

At Beyond Law Group I led five brands, four of them regulated businesses, growing turnover and headcount significantly in FY26 through a buy-and-build strategy combining M&A with organic growth across the group.

Select Property Group

Executive Director of a business that develops, sells and operates UK living-sector investments for funds, family offices and high-net-worth clients, through the delivery of market-leading residential brands. Select has transacted £1.82bn of UK investments.

Stockport County FC

As Executive Director I was part of acquiring the club in non-league and professionalising it on and off the pitch, on its way to League One. It was a true “learning on the job” experience for me and quite a few of the team!

Seventeen years as a corporate and private equity lawyer

After starting out in 1997, I moved to DLA Piper, then became an Equity Partner at Halliwells, acting on private equity transactions, M&A and joint ventures (often with a real estate or technology focus) in the UK and internationally. In a similar role at Shoosmiths I led the growth of the commercial division in Manchester, taking it from £1m to £11.5m turnover and from 30 to 150 people.

What you can expect

Clients bring me in when the stakes are high and the answer isn't obvious. These are the four things they tend to value.

Leading the adviser team

I've led teams in private practice and in-house, and spent years instructing and managing external lawyers, agents, lenders and financial advisers on major transactions. I don't replace your advisers. I make sure they stay focused, coordinated and good value.

Direct and accountable

You deal with me throughout, and I'll tell you what you need to hear, plainly. Where the role calls for it, I'll sit on your board, lead the negotiation or run the workstream myself, rather than just write a report.

A network built over nearly 30 years

Relationships across institutional investors, lenders, private equity, developers, operators and the professional advisers who work with them, used on your behalf where it helps.

Commercial first, with a lawyer's eye

I trained as a lawyer before going on to run businesses and invest in them. I approach every deal commercially, but I know where value gets lost between the heads of terms and the final documents, and I make sure what's agreed is what gets signed.

Mark Dawson

For more than a decade I've worked in executive and board roles. Most recently I was Managing Director and then Group CEO of Beyond Law Group, a private equity-backed portfolio of five professional services brands, where turnover and headcount grew significantly in FY26.

Before that I spent ten years on Vita Group's main board as Executive Director and Chief Legal Officer. My role combined group leadership with investment: strategy, acquisitions, funding, joint ventures, governance and exits across Vita Student, Vita Living, CitySuites, Union, Affinity Living and Symphony Park, with parallel roles at Select Property Group and Stockport County FC. I led group functions spanning legal, compliance, risk and people, and ran the external legal, agency and funding teams on the group's major transactions.

As a qualified solicitor, I still hold a current practising certificate. I spent the first part of my career as a corporate and private equity lawyer, including at DLA Piper, before becoming an Equity Partner at both Halliwells and Shoosmiths. That background means I can take a transaction from first structure to completion, on both the commercial and the legal side, and get the best from the lawyers and advisers working on it.

I set up Silklands Advisory to bring that experience to a small number of clients, alongside my wider executive commitments. Silklands Advisory provides commercial and business advisory services, not legal advice. Where a matter needs a lawyer, I work alongside yours.

Away from work you'll find me snowboarding in the Alps, practising yoga, out on country walks with my family and our Rottweiler, Roxy, or coaching my daughter's football team.
